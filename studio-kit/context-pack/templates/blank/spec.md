## Pitch

A dot you can steer until the time runs out. Replace all of this with the game.

## Rules

- R1 (auto): Holding a direction moves the dot 2 pixels per frame. It stops at the edges of the screen.
- R2 (auto): A round lasts 1800 frames, and the score goes up by 1 every 60 frames. Then the game-over screen shows.
- R3 (auto): The best score is still there after the game restarts.
- R4 (you check): The dot is easy to see.

## Controls

- Player 1: stick moves the dot.
- START or A: start from the title screen, and go back to it after game over.
- ★ is the cabinet's button; games never use it.

## Look & sound

Dark background, a sky-blue dot. A "win" sound when the round ends.

## Assumptions

- A1: One round, because nothing else is decided yet.
