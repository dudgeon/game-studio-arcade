# Ring Racer

maker: C · with: — · parent: — · session: —

## Pitch

Drive round the ring road as many times as you can before the clock runs out. Stay off the grass: it's slow.

## Rules

- R1 (auto): Holding A speeds the car up by 0.03 pixels per frame each frame, up to 2 on the road.
  Letting go slows it by 0.02 each frame to a stop.
- R2 (auto): Holding left or right turns the car. The turning builds up by 0.004 each frame to 0.04
  a frame, and eases off the same way when the stick is let go.
- R3 (auto): On the grass the car's top speed is 0.8.
- R4 (auto): Each time the car goes all the way round the ring, that is a lap and scores 100.
- R5 (auto): A race lasts 3600 frames. Then the game-over screen shows within 30 frames.
- R6 (you check): The ground stretches away to the horizon and turns as you steer, and trees grow as they come near.

## Controls

- Player 1: A is the pedal. Stick left and right steers.
- START or A: start from the title screen, and go back to it after game over.
- ★ is the cabinet's button; games never use it.

## Look & sound

A blue sky in bands over green grass with flowers, a gray ring road with a checked start line, round trees, and a red car seen from behind that leans into turns. Far ground fades into the sky. "LAP 1 +100" and a "powerup" sound for each lap, a "win" sound when time is up.

## Assumptions

- A1: One car and no other racers, because the request didn't say.
- A2: Sixty seconds a race.
