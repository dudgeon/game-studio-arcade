# Frog Hop   A · null · sesn_mock_01

## Pitch
Hop a frog across a busy road without getting squished.

## Rules
- R1 (auto) Pressing up moves the frog one lane up.
- R2 (auto) A car touching the frog costs one life.
- R3 (auto) The frog starts with three lives; at zero the game is over.
- R4 (auto) Cars get faster every time the frog reaches the top.
- R5 (you check) The frog looks happy when it reaches the top.

## Controls
Stick moves the frog one lane or one step. START pauses.

## Look & sound
Green frog, bright cars, a jolly hop sound.

## Assumptions
- A1 Cars start at a medium speed (the player said "medium and it gets faster").

- v1 First version. Player asked for three lives at the plan step.
