teti

A modern block stacker by TitanPlayz100, changed for the arcade cabinet.

Geoff's version: the same game, and it keeps your score in Marathon and 2 Minutes.

The game

  • teti by TitanPlayz100, MIT licence (github.com/TitanPlayz100/teti, commit 1de7944). Its code is used here, changed for the cabinet.
  • Cabinet changes: Marathon and Zen modes, a game picker and pause and game-over screens for a joystick and buttons, a bigger board, and its own small animation helper.

Sound, music and pictures

  • Sound effects, the music ("Brick Parade") and the block skin, particle and icons are made for this cabinet by a script in its own repository, not taken from other games.

Libraries and fonts

  • PixiJS 8.23.0, MIT licence.
  • Montserrat (Julieta Ulanovsky and others) and Major Mono Display (Emre Parlak), SIL Open Font License 1.1.

Every file's origin and licence is listed in NOTICE.md beside the game.
